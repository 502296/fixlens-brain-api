Support

We’re here to ensure you have a smooth and confident experience with FixLens.

FixLens is designed to assist drivers by providing AI-guided insights that help them better understand potential vehicle issues. Our support team is available to clarify usage questions, technical concerns, and general guidance related to the app.

How to contact us

When reaching out, a short explanation of what you were trying to do helps us respond more efficiently. You may include:

  • A brief description of the situation
  • The type of input used (text, image, or audio)
  • Any relevant context that may help clarify the request

Safety & responsible use

FixLens provides informational guidance only. It does not replace professional mechanical inspection, repair, or certification.

If you notice critical safety conditions such as smoke, strong fuel odors, overheating, or loss of vehicle control, discontinue use and seek professional assistance immediately.
