Support

We’re here to help you get the best experience with FixLens. If you have questions about how to use the app, need help understanding a feature, or want to report a technical issue, contact us anytime.

FixLens provides AI-assisted automotive guidance based on the information you choose to share (text, images, or audio). Our goal is to help you understand possible causes, organize your next steps, and communicate more clearly with a mechanic — in a calm, professional, and user-friendly way.

Responsible use & safety

FixLens is designed to provide informational guidance that may help you understand potential vehicle issues. It does not perform physical inspections, confirm a diagnosis with certainty, or authorize repairs.

If you observe urgent safety conditions — such as smoke, strong fuel odors, overheating, or loss of braking/steering control — stop driving when safe to do so and seek professional assistance immediately.

Tip: Many users share FixLens results with a mechanic to speed up the conversation and reduce unnecessary guesswork.
