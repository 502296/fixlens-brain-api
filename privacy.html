Privacy Policy

Your trust matters. This policy explains how FixLens handles information when you use the app and related services. FixLens is designed with a simple principle: collect less, protect more, and stay transparent.

Last updated: December 30, 2025

Information you choose to provide

FixLens may process the information you submit in order to generate AI-assisted guidance. You may choose to provide:

  • Text (symptoms, questions, notes)
  • Images (dashboard lights, parts, visible symptoms)
  • Audio (engine noises, idle behavior, unusual sounds)

How we use information

We use submitted information only for purposes that support the FixLens experience:

  • To generate diagnostic guidance and recommended next steps
  • To improve stability and reliability (for example: identifying errors or performance issues)
FixLens is not built for advertising or profiling. The focus is on helpful guidance and a professional user experience.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data
  • We do not use your content to build advertising profiles
  • We do not share your content beyond what is needed to operate the service

Permissions

FixLens uses device permissions only when initiated by you:

  • Camera / Photos — when you attach an image
  • Microphone — when you record audio

Data handling & retention

FixLens is designed to minimize data retention. Optional local “memory” features (if enabled in the app) are stored on your device. Submitted inputs may be processed temporarily to generate a response.

Where trusted third-party service providers are used, they support core operation and reliability only.
