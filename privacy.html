Privacy Policy

Your trust matters. This policy explains how FixLens handles information.

FixLens AI respects user privacy and is designed with data minimization and transparency in mind. This policy describes how information is handled when you choose to use the FixLens application and related services.

Information you choose to provide

  • Text you enter describing symptoms, questions, or observations
  • Images you upload for visual reference
  • Audio recordings you submit for sound-based guidance

How information is used

  • To generate AI-assisted diagnostic guidance
  • To improve application stability and reliability

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data
  • We do not use user content for advertising or profiling
  • We do not share data beyond what is required to operate the service

Permissions & access

Device permissions are used only when initiated by the user:

  • Camera / Photos — when attaching an image
  • Microphone — when recording audio

Data handling

FixLens is designed to limit data retention. Any optional local memory remains on the user’s device. Submitted inputs may be processed temporarily to generate responses.
