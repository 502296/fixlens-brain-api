Safety & Scope

FixLens is built to support drivers with calm, practical, professional guidance. This page explains what FixLens can do, what it cannot do, and how to use it responsibly.

What FixLens is

FixLens is an AI-assisted automotive guidance tool. It helps interpret user-provided information such as symptoms described in text, visible clues in photos, and sound patterns in audio recordings.

The goal is clarity — helping you understand possibilities and next steps, not replacing professional inspection.

What FixLens is not

FixLens does not perform physical inspection, does not confirm a diagnosis with certainty, and does not guarantee safety outcomes. Vehicles can present similar symptoms for different reasons, and some issues require hands-on testing.

  • No physical inspection or repair
  • No guarantee of accuracy or final diagnosis
  • No replacement for a qualified mechanic

When to seek professional help immediately

If you notice any urgent or safety-critical condition, stop using FixLens and seek professional assistance. Examples include:

  • Smoke, burning smell, or signs of fire
  • Strong fuel odor, visible fuel leak, or suspected fuel system issue
  • Overheating, coolant loss, or temperature warning
  • Loss of braking, steering control, or severe vibration at speed
  • Sudden power loss that makes driving unsafe

How to use FixLens effectively

FixLens works best when you provide clear, simple inputs. Many users use FixLens to prepare before visiting a mechanic, which can help reduce confusion and speed up the conversation.

  • Text: describe when the issue happens (idle, accelerating, turning, braking)
  • Photo: include dashboard lights, warning messages, or visible leaks if safe
  • Audio: record the sound in a safe environment, as close as possible to the source

You remain in control: FixLens offers guidance; you choose what to do next.

Scope of guidance

FixLens provides general guidance and educational explanations. It may suggest common causes, checks to consider, and questions to ask a mechanic. It is not intended for emergency response or safety-critical decisions.
