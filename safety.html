Safety & Scope

This page explains what FixLens can do, what it cannot do, and how to use it responsibly.

What FixLens is

FixLens is an AI-assisted automotive guidance tool that helps interpret text, photos, and audio you provide.

The goal is clarity, not replacing professional inspection.

When to seek professional help immediately

  • Smoke, burning smell, or signs of fire
  • Strong fuel odor or visible leaks
  • Overheating or temperature warnings
  • Loss of brakes/steering control
  • Sudden power loss that makes driving unsafe

How to use FixLens effectively

  • Text: when it happens (idle, acceleration, turning, braking)
  • Photo: dashboard lights, warnings, visible leaks (if safe)
  • Audio: record in a safe place near the sound source
